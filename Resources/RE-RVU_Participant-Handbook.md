## 01. Welcome to RE:RVU
*(Handbook Page 03)*

> **"What if you could help shape the digital systems used across your university?"**

**RVU Connect** was developed by Iterium for the Office of Student Affairs at RV University, with the objective of improving the university’s digital experience and making its services more accessible to the student community.

But building a platform is only the beginning. Its real value lies in how effectively it serves the people who use it.

**RE:RVU v1.0** is a seven-day, student-led product innovation initiative by Iterium. It invites students to rethink, improve, and expand RVU Connect while exploring new digital solutions for the wider university ecosystem.

This initiative is built around a simple idea: **students should not only experience the systems around them but also have opportunities to contribute to making those systems better.**

Over seven days, participants will identify problems, develop solutions, and demonstrate their ideas through prototypes.

The strongest contributions may be considered for further development through Iterium’s product initiatives.

> **Key Takeaway:**  
> The goal is not just to submit an idea. It is to **demonstrate what could actually be built**.

## 02. Understanding the Two Tracks
*(Handbook Pages 04–05)*

Participants must select exactly one of the following two tracks:

---

### Track 01 — RVU Connect Innovation
> **Focus:** *Make the existing platform genuinely useful.*

RVU Connect already has several core features and workflows. The challenge is to improve the overall experience and make the platform more intuitive, accessible, and valuable to its users.

Explore the existing platform and evaluate how effectively users can navigate and interact with it.

#### You May Focus On:

1. **User Experience:**
   * Simplifying confusing user journeys.
   * Reducing unnecessary steps.
   * Improving the overall interaction experience.
   * Making important information easier to find.

2. **Interface and Design:**
   * Improving visual hierarchy.
   * Creating more intuitive layouts.
   * Improving consistency across screens.
   * Making interfaces easier to understand and navigate.

3. **Navigation and Information Architecture:**
   * Organising information more effectively.
   * Improving navigation structures.
   * Making important sections easier to access.
   * Reducing friction between related workflows.

4. **Existing Functionality:**
   * Improving the usefulness of existing features.
   * Identifying gaps in current workflows.
   * Making existing capabilities more reliable and accessible.
   * Improving how users complete common tasks.

#### What We Expect:
* Your proposal should identify a genuine limitation or opportunity within RVU Connect and demonstrate how your changes improve the experience.
* **Important:** Adding more features is not automatically an improvement. Prioritise **usefulness, simplicity, and user value**.

#### Live Platform URL:
* Access RVU Connect at: [`https://rvu-connect.vercel.app/?route=home`](https://rvu-connect.vercel.app/?route=home)
* Refer to the *RVU Connect Product Exploration Guide* for a structured approach to exploring the platform.

---

### Track 02 — Open Innovation
> **Focus:** *Identify a problem. Build a solution.*

The university ecosystem consists of students, faculty, departments, administrative teams, campus services, and multiple interconnected processes. Many of these experiences can be improved through better software, automation, and product thinking.

Your task is to identify a meaningful problem within the university ecosystem and propose a digital solution.

#### Potential Areas Include (Suggestions, Not Restrictions):
* Academic and administrative workflows.
* Department-level operations.
* Student services and campus facilities.
* Communication and information access.
* University events and activities.
* Repetitive manual processes.
* Campus-wide digital infrastructure.

> You may identify a problem not listed above, provided it has a meaningful connection to the RV University ecosystem.

#### What We Expect:
Your submission must clearly demonstrate:
1. **01 — Problem:** A clearly defined problem.
2. **02 — Users:** A specific group of users experiencing that problem.
3. **03 — Solution:** A practical solution addressing the problem.
4. **04 — Mechanics:** A clear explanation of how the solution works.
5. **05 — Prototype:** A prototype demonstrating the proposed experience.

*Your solution may be developed as an independent product or proposed for potential integration into the wider RVU Connect ecosystem where appropriate.*

## 04. What You Must Submit
*(Handbook Pages 07–08)*

Every individual participant or team is required to submit **two mandatory deliverables**:

### Deliverable 01 — Solution Deck
* **Format:** Submitted exclusively as a **PDF document**.
* **Objective:** Communicate your identified problem, proposed solution, user experience, and implementation approach with clarity and structure.

#### The Deck Must Cover (8 Required Sections):

1. **Problem Statement:** What specific problem are you addressing? Explain the issue clearly, avoiding vague generalities.
2. **Target Users:** Who experiences this problem? Clearly identify the primary stakeholders or user groups who benefit.
3. **Problem Context:** Why does this problem matter? Detail its direct impact on university operations or the student experience.
4. **Proposed Solution:** Describe your solution concept and explain exactly how it resolves the identified problem.
5. **Key Features:** Detail the most critical capabilities and user-facing workflows of your solution.
6. **User Journey:** Show step-by-step how a user interacts with your solution, from initial entry to task completion.
7. **Implementation Approach:** Explain how your solution could realistically be engineered, developed, and deployed, including requirements.
8. **Expected Impact:** Quantify or explain the measurable improvement in efficiency, satisfaction, or student experience.

> **Design Tip:** The deck should be concise, well-structured, and supported by visual mockups, diagrams, and flowcharts wherever possible.

---

### Deliverable 02 — Prototype
* **Format:** A public, accessible web or interactive link demonstrating your proposed solution.
* **Objective:** Enable evaluators to experience and interact with the solution rather than merely reading an abstract concept.

#### Accepted Prototype Formats:
* Working web application (live hosted URL).
* Working mobile application (live build / demo link).
* Interactive UI/UX design prototype.
* Clickable Figma prototype (must have public view permissions enabled).
* Functional technical proof of concept (PoC).

> **Crucial Rule:** A static idea description or slide deck without a functioning/interactive prototype is **insufficient** and will not qualify.

---

### Submission Requirements Checklist:
* [x] Submit your final solution deck in **PDF format**.
* [x] Provide a working, accessible prototype link.
* [x] Ensure that external links do **not** require login credentials or special access permissions from evaluators.
* [x] Set Figma sharing permissions to *"Anyone with the link can view"*.
* [x] Clearly articulate the individual contributions of each team member.
* [x] Submit before the published deadline on Unstop.
* [ ] *GitHub repository:* **Not required**.
* [ ] *Video walkthrough:* **Not required** (optional).

## 05. Use of AI Tools
*(Handbook Page 09)*

AI tools and modern assistive technologies are **permitted throughout the entire initiative**.

### Permitted Uses of AI:
* Research and problem exploration.
* Ideation, brainstorming, and solution planning.
* UI/UX layout and visual generation.
* Rapid prototyping and interactive mockup creation.
* Application code development and automation scripting.
* Code testing, debugging, and refinement.
* Documentation and slide deck presentation drafting.

### Accountability Mandate:
Using AI does not diminish your responsibility to deeply understand your submission. Participants must be fully prepared to defend and explain:
* The core problem they identified.
* Why the proposed solution is appropriate and effective.
* The internal mechanics and architecture of their prototype.
* Individual and team contributions to the project.

> **Standard:** Submissions must reflect **genuine human understanding and meaningful execution**, not unedited, boilerplate AI outputs.

## 06. Evaluation Framework
*(Handbook Pages 10–11)*

All valid submissions will be systematically assessed across five core criteria, each carrying equal weight:

### Core Evaluation Criteria & Weightage

| # | Evaluation Criterion | Weightage |
| :---: | :--- | :---: |
| 1 | **Problem Understanding and Relevance** | 20% |
| 2 | **Innovation and Solution Quality** | 20% |
| 3 | **User Experience and Design** | 20% |
| 4 | **Feasibility and Technical Potential** | 20% |
| 5 | **Prototype Quality and Execution** | 20% |
| **Total** | **Comprehensive Score** | **100%** |

---

### Detailed Assessment Dimensions:

1. **Problem Understanding and Relevance (20%):**
   * Is the problem clearly articulated and well-defined?
   * Does the submission demonstrate a deep understanding of the underlying operational or user issue?
   * Is the issue genuinely relevant to the university community?

2. **Innovation and Solution Quality (20%):**
   * Is the proposed approach thoughtful, creative, and purposeful?
   * Does it address the root cause of the problem effectively?
   * Does it deliver meaningful user value beyond superficial feature additions?

3. **User Experience and Design (20%):**
   * Is the interface intuitive and easy to use?
   * Are navigation workflows clear, streamlined, and logical?
   * Does the visual design effectively support the overall user experience?

4. **Feasibility and Technical Potential (20%):**
   * Can the proposed solution realistically be engineered and maintained?
   * Are technical and data requirements reasonable and scalable?
   * Does the solution possess practical viability for real-world campus deployment?

5. **Prototype Quality and Execution (20%):**
   * Does the prototype effectively demonstrate the proposed solution?
   * Are core user journeys and workflows fully interactable?
   * Is the prototype sufficiently complete and stable to evaluate?

---

### Track-Specific Evaluation Focus:

* **Track 01 (RVU Connect Innovation):**  
  Evaluators will pay special attention to **meaningful improvements to existing features, information architecture, navigation, usability, and friction reduction** across existing user journeys.
* **Track 02 (Open Innovation):**  
  Evaluators will focus on the **significance and depth of the problem identified, clarity of target user profiles, practicality of the architectural solution, and viability of institutional implementation**.

## 07. What Happens After RE:RVU?
*(Handbook Page 11)*

RE:RVU is designed as the launchpad for continued product development, rather than a standalone hackathon.

### Post-Event Pathways:
Following evaluation, select standout submissions will be considered for formal product and technical review by **Iterium**.

Depending on technical feasibility, institutional approvals, and resource allocation, selected contributions may be:
* Further developed into standalone, independent production applications.
* Formally integrated as modules within the official **RVU Connect** platform.
* Refined and iterated in direct collaboration with the original student contributors.
* Scheduled for campus-wide beta deployment across the university ecosystem.

> **Governance Notice:** Selection during RE:RVU does not automatically guarantee implementation, integration, or deployment. All further engineering is subject to formal administrative reviews and RSST/RVU approvals.

### Opportunities Beyond the Event:
* **Product Collaboration:** Participants with high-potential submissions may join Iterium on subsequent engineering and product cycles.
* **Paid Project Opportunities:** High-performing contributors may be considered for future paid client and internal product projects undertaken by Iterium, subject to project availability, individual vetting, and mutually agreed terms.

## 09. Participation Guidelines
*(Handbook Page 13)*

To maintain equity, academic integrity, and operational order, all participants must comply with the following 10 regulations:

1. **Eligibility:** Participation is open exclusively to bona fide students currently enrolled at RV University.
2. **Mandatory Submissions:** Every participant or team must submit both a PDF Solution Deck and an accessible Prototype link.
3. **Team Configuration:** Participants may register as individuals or in teams, subject to team configuration limits supported on Unstop.
4. **Track Exclusivity:** Each submission must be registered under exactly one of the two designated tracks (Track 01 or Track 02).
5. **Deadline Compliance:** All materials must be uploaded to Unstop before the strictly enforced published deadline.
6. **Link Accessibility:** Participants are solely responsible for ensuring prototype links and shared files remain accessible to evaluators without authentication barriers.
7. **Intellectual Property Rights:** Participants must hold the right to demonstrate and present all submitted designs, assets, and code.
8. **Third-Party Attribution:** Any external APIs, open-source libraries, design kits, or assets used must be properly cited and credited.
9. **Academic Integrity:** Submitting or misrepresenting another person’s work as your own is strictly prohibited and results in immediate disqualification.
10. **Administrative Right:** The organising committee reserves the right to request technical demonstrations, source code reviews, or clarifications at any time.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
*(Handbook Page 14)*

* **Q01: Can I participate individually?**  
  **Yes.** Both individual participants and teams are eligible to participate.

* **Q02: Can I choose either track?**  
  **Yes.** You may choose either Track 01 (RVU Connect Innovation) or Track 02 (Open Innovation), but your submission must align with only one track.

* **Q03: Do I have to build a completely new application?**  
  **No.** Track 01 focuses on improving the existing RVU Connect platform. Track 02 focuses on proposing a digital solution to a university-related problem.

* **Q04: Can I submit only an idea?**  
  **No.** Submissions require both a structured PDF Solution Deck and an interactive Prototype link. Purely conceptual text entries will not be evaluated.

* **Q05: Does my prototype need to be fully functional with a backend?**  
  **No.** It must demonstrate the proposed solution sufficiently for evaluation. An interactive, clickable UI/UX design (e.g., Figma) or a frontend proof of concept is completely acceptable.

* **Q06: Can I use AI tools during the event?**  
  **Yes.** AI-assisted research, ideation, design, coding, and documentation are permitted. You must understand and be able to explain your work.

* **Q07: Do I need to submit a GitHub repository?**  
  **No.** A public code repository is not mandatory for submission.

* **Q08: Do I need to submit a demo video?**  
  **No.** A recorded video walkthrough is optional and not compulsory.

* **Q09: Will every selected solution be deployed?**  
  **No.** Selected entries undergo additional technical and administrative feasibility reviews. Deployment depends on approvals, resources, and technical alignment.

* **Q10: Can my solution become part of RVU Connect?**  
  **Potentially.** Standout features and workflows aligned with university priorities may be considered for integration into the platform.

* **Q11: Will everyone receive a certificate?**  
  **Yes.** All eligible participants who submit a complete, valid deliverable before the deadline will receive a Certificate of Participation.

* **Q12: Will there be opportunities after the event?**  
  **Yes.** Selected contributors may be invited to collaborate with Iterium on product builds and considered for future paid project opportunities.
